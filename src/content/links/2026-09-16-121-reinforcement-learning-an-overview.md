A comprehensive survey of reinforcement learning by Kevin P. Murphy (Google DeepMind), covering value-based, policy-based, model-based, and multi-agent RL, plus the intersection of RL and LLMs (RLHF, RLVR, PPO, GRPO, DPO) and topics like exploration, imitation learning, and offline RL.
