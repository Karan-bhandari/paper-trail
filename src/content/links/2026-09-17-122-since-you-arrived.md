A "memento mori for the internet age" — a live, single-page counter that tallies what the world has gained and lost (births, deaths, songs heard, meals shared, carbon emitted, species vanished, etc.) during the minutes you've spent on the page, with sourced figures from the UN, IFPI, SIPRI, IUCN, and more.
